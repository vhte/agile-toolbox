Team tools that respect the room.

Nine tools for the work around the work: running ceremonies, forecasting honestly, and writing down the rules a team of people and AI agents now needs. They run entirely in your browser. Why it is built this way

Privacy is a facilitation feature

People self-censor when they suspect a tool remembers. Nothing here is transmitted, so that suspicion has nowhere to land. Your data lives in this browser until you export or delete it.

Never measure individuals

Turn timers help a facilitator keep a meeting inside its box and are never stored per person. Blockers aggregate by category. Nothing here can be turned into a performance report, by design.

Ranges, not single numbers

The capacity planner and the flow forecaster refuse to produce one figure, because one figure becomes a promise. You get a forecast with a confidence attached, to say out loud.

Your data is portable

Every tool exports JSON that imports into any other copy, and Markdown that pastes into a ticket or a wiki. Nothing is trapped, so nothing needs a migration plan.

Your data

A backup is a single JSON file covering every tool. Restoring merges it into what is already here, which is useful for moving between machines, or for handing a team's history to whoever takes over facilitation. Clearing your browser's site data does the same thing as the erase button, which is worth knowing before you rely on this for anything you cannot lose.
