Decision Log

Decisions now happen in chat threads, calls and AI sessions, and the reasoning scrolls away with them. Record the decision and the reason in one line, expand it into a lightweight ADR when it matters, and hand the index to newcomers and AI agents.

Press Enter. The text before the first "because" becomes the decision, the rest becomes the rationale. It is saved as Proposed; open it later to add options and consequences.

    How to use this
    • What deserves a record: anything a new teammate would find arbitrary in six months without the reasoning, and anything decided on incomplete information that should be revisited. Skip the trivially reversible.
    • Capture first, polish later. One line in the thread or at the end of the call ("We decided X because Y") beats a perfect ADR nobody writes. Expand the important ones afterwards.
    • The fields follow MADR (Markdown Any Decision Records), itself a descendant of Michael Nygard's Architecture Decision Records: context and problem, decision drivers, options with pros and cons, the chosen option and why, and consequences.
    • Decision owner is the person or role accountable for the decision and for revisiting it. It is ownership, not a metric: this tool never counts decisions per person.
    • Never edit history; supersede it. "Supersede with new decision" drafts a linked record. The old decision stays Accepted until the new one is Accepted, so a rejected proposal never leaves the team without a decision.
    • Revisit by plus Confidence make low-confidence calls visible: Accepted records on or past their date get a "Revisit due" badge.
    • AI-proposed decisions are fine when a human accepts them; mark the origin so the team knows to check the reasoning. The "Index for AGENTS.md" export gives agents and newcomers the accepted decisions in a few lines.
    • Import JSON merges records into the selected log. Numbers already used (or retired) in that log are renumbered, and the tool tells you which.
